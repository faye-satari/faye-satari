## About Me

I am a Software Developer and Computer Engineer with a background spanning
software development, data visualization, databases, Internet of Things,
connected systems, and applied research.

I hold a Master of Applied Science (M.A.Sc.) in Computer Engineering from
Polytechnique Montréal.

My graduate research focused on the design of a secure and scalable
remote-health-monitoring architecture using wearable Internet of Medical
Things (IoMT), 5G networks, and smart-city infrastructure.

My broader technical and research interests include:

- Software Engineering
- Internet of Things (IoT) and Internet of Medical Things (IoMT)
- Smart Cities and Connected Systems
- 5G and Telecommunication Systems
- Data Visualization
- Databases and Data Systems
- Backend APIs
- Software and Data Security
